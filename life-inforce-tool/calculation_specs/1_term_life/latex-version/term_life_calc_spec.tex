\section*{INTRODUCTION}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{INTRODUCTION}

This document provides a comprehensive overview of the formulas for Inforce Term products. The business rules are not considered in this document.

It is divided into 4 parts:

\begin{enumerate}
\item[1)] \textbf{Duration:} This section details the formulas of the various durations used in the calculations
\item[2)] \textbf{Input Setup:} This section details the inputs we get from Capsil, and the various calculations related to those inputs.
\item[3)] \textbf{Report:} This section details the formulas for each variable appearing in the Illustration Report (projection values). For report purposes, \underline{the projections are calculated as of the date of the next policy anniversary}.
\end{enumerate}

The coverages included in the calculations are all the term products and their additional guarantees. \section{REPORT: Age}

\underline{For the global illustration / policy:}

\[
{Age}_{\left(iDur\right)} = Illustration\ Minimum\ Age + iDur
\]

$\rightarrow$ Where iDur is between \underline{\textit{Policy 1st Duration}} and \underline{\textit{End of Illustration Projection}}

\underline{For the coverages:}

\[
{Age}_{\left(iCov,iDur\right)} = {Coverage\ Minimum\ Age}_{\left(iCov\right)} + iDur
\]

$\rightarrow$ Where iDur is between \underline{\textit{Coverage 1st Duration}} and \underline{\textit{End of Coverage Projection}}

\[
Illustration\ Minimum\ Age = \operatorname{\underset{1\leq\ iCov\ \leq Nb\ of\ Cov.\ on\ illustration}{MIN}}{\left\{{Coverage\ Minimum\ Age}_{\left(iCov\right)}\right\}}
\]

\]

\[

\subsection{Guidelines per coverage}

\begin{itemize}
  \item The \textit{CoverageNetPremiumReserve} must always be calculated for all coverages since their \underline{inception}.
  \item For each coverage, the calculation is based on the illustration \underline{coverage parameters}, which are applied retrospectively to calculate the \textit{CoverageNetPremiumReserve} from inception across all durations.
  \item The \textit{CoverageNetPremiumReserve} is calculated on the anniversary date of each individual coverage, and not on the policy anniversary date
  \item For each coverage, it must be determined whether the first duration displayed in the report projection should be deferred. Deferral applies when \underline{the policy was issued before December 31, while the coverage was issued after December 31}.
\end{itemize}

\[
{Coverage\ Net\ Premium\ Reserve}_{\left(iCov,\ iDur\right)}=\sum_{iInsured=1}^{NbInsuredOnCov}{Net\ Premium\ Reserve}_{\left(iCov,iInsured,iDur\right)}
